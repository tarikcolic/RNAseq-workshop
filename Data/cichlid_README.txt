## Sample naming

Sample names follow the pattern `<species><age><light><replicate>`, e.g. `cit118r3`:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `cit` | *Amphilophus citrinellus* |
| `ast` | *Amphilophus astorquii* |
| `7` | Age of 7 days |
| `118` | Age of 118 days |
| `r` | Raised under **red** light |
| `w` | Raised under **white** light |
| trailing number | Replicate individual |

## Tasks

The species A. Astorquii is derived from A. Citronellas about 3000 generations ago and lives in clear waters, while the ancestor (a. Citronellas) lives in turbid waters. What has changed in the eyes of the fish in clear waters compared to turbid ones? How does the environmental influence gene expression in the eyes of these fish? Does the environment have different effects on the two species or do they react the same way? How does the environment change development?
